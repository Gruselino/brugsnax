    SnackVotesQtyPrice
    Prices are the median of the prices recorded for that product on Open Prices (EUR; ≈ means converted from another currency at today's ECB rate). Pick a candidate that shows a price; products without one count as 0 €. Click a price to type your own or to refresh the median. Qty = how many to buy; the total is price × qty.
